# Wish (Android) — review analysis

> Persisted by the coordinator, with the user's approval, from the isolated analyst's returned findings: the harness blocked subagent writes of report files. The content is the worker's, not the coordinator's.

Sample: 101 Google Play reviews, labels gb/il/us; no Israel-specific or Hebrew signal.

## 1. User and job
Bargain shoppers buying generic low-cost goods.

## 2. What wins
Generic loyalty and value praise (the loved share may be inflated by the rating-sort stratum).

## 3. What breaks
1. **Orders never arriving, or taking weeks to months** (cluster co-occurrence; pain segment of 50 of 101 sampled reviews; all markets).
2. **Refund and money-back failures** (refund cluster 13, money 13). The failure type is unclear (slice requested).
3. Weaker: suspected bait pricing on deal events, login and load failures in recent versions, catalogue decay into generic items with churn to Temu.

## 4. Feature use and unmet jobs
Unmet: reliable arrival, and refunds back to the original payment card.

## 5. Potential wedge
A Hebrew-first, single-niche curated store with delivery dates to Israel stated up front and refunds to the original card. Confidence low to moderate.

## 6. Confidence and falsification
Themes rest mostly on phrase-cluster co-occurrence rather than visible text. The refund slice is pending. Falsify: if refund and arrival complaints are rare in a country-routed Israeli sample, drop the reliability wedge.
