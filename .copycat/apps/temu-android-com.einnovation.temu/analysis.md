# Temu (Android) — review analysis

> Persisted by the coordinator, with the user's approval, from the isolated analyst's returned findings: the harness blocked subagent writes of report files. The content is the worker's, not the coordinator's.

Sample: 71 Google Play reviews; 65/71 multi-market; no Hebrew reviews; many dated 2026-11-12 (pre-Black Friday).

## 1. User and job
Price-driven shoppers after cheap, varied goods, drawn in by promotions.

## 2. What wins
Price, variety, stage-by-stage tracking.

## 3. What breaks
1. **Distrust of promotions** (6+ reviews, all market labels, high helpfulness): free-gift, reward, coupon and game mechanics feel manipulative.
2. **Credit and cash-back confusion** (moderate).
3. **Cart vs. checkout price inconsistency** (low to moderate; one dominant review).
4. **Slow or late delivery** (low to moderate).

## 4. Feature use and unmet jobs
Unmet: an honest, all-in price and a delivery date the shopper can trust, without games.

## 5. Potential wedge
A curated niche store for Israeli and English-speaking shoppers: all-in price in ₪/$, no gamified promotions, an honest delivery date.

## 6. Confidence and falsification
The "free" cluster is ambiguous (free shipping vs. free gifts) and unverified. Market labels are not independent. Falsify with a transparency-vs-cheapest message split test on a landing page.
