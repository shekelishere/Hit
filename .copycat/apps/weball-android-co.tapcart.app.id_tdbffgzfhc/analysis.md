# WE BALL (Android) — review analysis

> Persisted by the coordinator, with the user's approval, from the isolated analyst's returned findings: the harness blocked subagent writes of report files. The content is the worker's, not the coordinator's.

Sample: only 7 reviews (2024-07-12 to 2026-09-02; ratings 4×5, 1×4, 1×2, 1×1); one global English corpus. **Very low confidence.**

## 1. User and job
Buyers of sports gear (gloves, American football) from a single-brand Tapcart storefront.

## 2. What wins
Deals and the products themselves (not app features); positive reviews are mostly one or two words.

## 3. What breaks
1. Size exchange for gloves with no live human support, and money lost on the exchange. Newest review, version 19, 2 stars (Android review 0915c24e-ffb5-41ba-ae38-fe29d23e77df).
2. An order apparently not delivered, 1 star (5e2e86a1-219a-45b1-a361-9ec8c41ef2e3).
3. Mild payment friction (3adb7827-b4e4-4567-8754-9bce1af285c8, 4 stars).

## 4. Feature use and unmet jobs
Unmet: easy fit and size exchange, and human support.

## 5. Potential wedge
Fit-sensitive sports gear with free size exchange, human chat in Hebrew and English, and tracked delivery. Unvalidated: each theme rests on a single review.

## 6. Confidence and falsification
Compare size-exchange and human-support complaints across the larger corpora. If they are rare next to price and shipping speed, drop the fit-and-exchange wedge.
