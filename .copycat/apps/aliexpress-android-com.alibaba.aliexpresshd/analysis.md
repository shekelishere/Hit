# AliExpress (Android) — review analysis

> Persisted by the coordinator, with the user's approval, from the isolated analyst's returned findings: the harness blocked subagent writes of report files. The content is the worker's, not the coordinator's.

Sample: 75 deduplicated Google Play reviews; market labels il/us/gb (71/75 multi-market; effectively one global English corpus, no Hebrew reviews).

## 1. User and job
Price-driven shoppers buying a wide range of low-cost goods directly from Chinese sellers. For Israball, this is the status-quo workaround: buy direct and accept the risks.

## 2. What wins
Low prices and wide range (table stakes); shipping mentioned positively at cluster level only; an easy app. A reseller cannot win on price.

## 3. What breaks
1. **Seller trust (moderate confidence):** items don't match photos or descriptions, fake reviews, confusing multi-variant listings. Android GB-first reviews 569970ca-6f5b-40f5-8785-589343ab7777, cafeb8c1-e34f-4ce6-a1f1-fb45b3333089, e2a99a7f-bc21-4f5d-89e3-108b40f861f2.
2. **Returns, disputes and support:** bot support, return postage charged for wrong or faulty items, no resolution. Reviews dca77412-a9ae-43a8-8c4d-13ebe0cfa677, 569970ca-…, 5af78570-9a6b-40ce-9fca-121173d0173e.
3. **Tracking:** appears in 4 pain-cluster reviews (504312a5-…, d939e9c8-…, 796e3c8b-…, 6f274a3d-…); the failure mode is unverified (slice requested).
4. **App UX:** navigation jumps, slowdown, cart failures, gamification clutter (c497e084-…, 0b9912a7-…, 6b976f15-…). Real, but not core to a reseller.

## 4. Feature use and unmet jobs
Unmet: confidence that an item matches its listing; accountable human returns; clear delivery status.

## 5. Potential wedge
Hebrew-speaking shoppers in Israel buying one curated category. Promise: vetted items that match the listing, delivery status for Israel, prices in ₪, and a local human-handled return without international return postage.

## 6. Confidence and falsification
No Israel-specific evidence (market parameters are not country-routed). The 5-star stratum may be over-sampled. Tracking slice pending. Falsify: if Israeli shoppers rank price far above trust and returns in a landing-page test, the wedge fails.
