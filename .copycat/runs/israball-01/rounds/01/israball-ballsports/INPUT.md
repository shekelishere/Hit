## Table stakes

Competitive prices versus marketplace references (not the lever); free shipping above a low threshold; stage-by-stage tracking; a refund for non-delivery, damage or items not as described, with a window of about 90 days; ILS pricing; accounts and fast checkout. Source: 03-market-evidence.md §7.

## Differentiation

A vetted, small ball-sports catalogue whose photos and sizes match what arrives (an Israball-measured size guide); a human Israel-side size exchange and return without international return postage; refund to the original card; Hebrew-first UI and service. Evidence: review P2 and P3 (03-review-evidence.md); the WE BALL size-exchange gap; no dedicated Israeli online ball-sport specialist found (03-market-evidence.md §2, §7).

## Distribution and monetization

Acquisition: Hebrew search and local football, basketball and padel communities (clubs, WhatsApp groups, youth leagues). Monetization: product margin at AliExpress cost plus a 25–40% trust premium; baskets kept under the VAT exemption.

## Counterevidence

Price is the main retention reason in every corpus; no trust premium is shown; Wish churn went to Temu for price; AliExpress local warehouses and Parcel Home are closing the speed gap; size-exchange evidence is one review; Israeli ball-sport demand is unmeasured; the reseller owns Buyer Protection disputes and return costs; wartime delivery shocks.

## Constraints

Website plus mobile version (iOS and Android). Sourcing from AliExpress (Dropshipping Center / Open Platform; Value-Added APIs gated). Original name (Israball), copy, assets and interaction design; no AliExpress, Temu or WE BALL branding, listing copy, photos or marks; no Temu-style games, coins or click-to-buy. Markets: il primary, us and gb secondary. Out of scope without separate approval: payments, ad spend, supplier or customer contact. Evidence limits: all four profiles are partial, the review corpus is global English Google Play (no Hebrew, not country-routed), and the Israeli VAT exemption threshold is unknown ($75 vs $130/$150).
