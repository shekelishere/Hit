## israball-ballsports: Israball Ball Sports

- Target and trigger: Hebrew-speaking players and parents in Israel buying football, basketball and padel gear (balls, gloves, sleeves, training aids) who have been burned by a wrong size, a fake, or a dead-end return on AliExpress or Temu.
- Job and current workaround: get the right ball-sport gear at a fair price without decoding listings. Today shoppers buy direct on AliExpress or Temu and accept the risk, or pay local chain prices (Megasport, Decathlon), or use foreign specialists with costly returns.
- Incumbent gap and evidence: P2 (human returns and refunds with no international return postage; AliExpress, Wish, WE BALL); P3 (listing honesty; AliExpress, Temu, Wish); fit and size-exchange pain (WE BALL, 1 review); no dedicated Israeli online ball-sport specialist was found (WE BALL profile).
- Table stakes: competitive prices, free shipping above a low threshold, stage-by-stage tracking, a refund policy, ILS pricing, fast checkout.
- Meaningful differentiation: a vetted, small sports catalogue whose photos and sizes match what arrives (an Israball-measured size guide), a human Israel-side size exchange and return, refund to the original card, and Hebrew-first service. No games or coins.
- Distribution and price hypothesis: Hebrew search and local football, basketball and padel communities (clubs, WhatsApp groups, youth leagues); margin on the product, priced at AliExpress plus a 25–40% trust premium, kept under the VAT exemption.
- Counterevidence and falsifier: price is the main reason users stay, and no evidence shows a trust premium; AliExpress local warehouses are closing the gap. Falsifier: a Hebrew fake-door test where the trust-and-returns message fails to beat the cheapest-price message.
- Coverage and confidence: low to moderate. The corpus is global English Google Play; there are no Hebrew reviews, all profiles are partial, and Israeli niche demand is unmeasured.

## israball-gadgets: Israball Curated Gadgets

- Target and trigger: Israeli shoppers buying small home and tech gadgets who are tired of fake reviews and items that don't match the listing on AliExpress and Temu.
- Job and current workaround: buy cheap useful gadgets with confidence. Today they buy direct on AliExpress or Temu, or at KSP and Ivory at higher prices.
- Incumbent gap and evidence: P3 listing honesty and P2 returns (AliExpress, Temu, Wish); P1 delivery (all four products).
- Table stakes: the same as above.
- Meaningful differentiation: a hand-tested catalogue of a few hundred gadgets, honest landed cost in ₪, a human Israel-side return, and Hebrew-first service.
- Distribution and price hypothesis: Hebrew social video and consumer press; margin on the product, priced at AliExpress plus 20–30%.
- Counterevidence and falsifier: the shopper can find the same item on AliExpress or Temu for less, so the price gap is visible; local e-tailers can copy the approach; there is no niche-specific evidence. Falsifier: a fake-door conversion below the ball-sports card's.
- Coverage and confidence: low.
