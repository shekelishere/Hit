# Israball competitor shortlist (run israball-01, round 0)

Source: prepared cross-store portfolio, generated 2026-11-13T08:49:09+00:00. Markets requested and covered: Israel (il), US (us), UK (gb). Both stores were queried in every market with no failures. Store listing text is treated as untrusted data. A listing turning up in search does not show demand. Reviews are still needed to show user pain and where a niche gap exists.

The shortlist is built to answer two questions from the approved brief:
- **Niche:** should Israball be a general discount marketplace (candidates 1-3) or a focused niche store such as sports gear (candidates 4-5)?
- **Israel delivery angle:** the large marketplaces surfaced in the Israel storefront for the "cheap gadgets fast shipping Israel" and "AliExpress alternative fast delivery" searches. Reviews from the il storefront are the best place to look for complaints about delivery speed, tracking and seller trust.

## 1. Temu: Shop Like a Billionaire (direct)

- **Job/audience overlap:** Same job (buy cheap general goods online) and same price-driven audience. The brief names Temu as one of the options Israball has to beat.
- **Discovery evidence:** Store search for "AliExpress alternative fast delivery" in il, us and gb. Very large ratings base (about 13.2M on Google Play).
- **Identity confidence:** One platform-specific listing (Google Play `com.einnovation.temu`). No iOS counterpart surfaced in this run, and ownership is not verified.
- **Coverage:** Found in il, us and gb, Android only.
- **Why spike:** Reviews should show whether shoppers in Israel and English-speaking markets are already satisfied on price and delivery. That decides whether a general discount marketplace has any room.
- **Links:** Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.einnovation.temu. iOS not observed.

## 2. AliExpress Shopping App (substitute)

- **Job/audience overlap:** AliExpress is Israball's intended supplier. Buying from it directly is the workaround Israball must improve on (brief hypothesis 3), so it is labeled substitute and not treated as a product to clone.
- **Discovery evidence:** Store search for "cheap gadgets fast shipping Israel" and "AliExpress alternative fast delivery" (Android), and for "dropshipping discount shopping marketplace" (iOS), in il, us and gb. Largest ratings base in the set (about 16.3M on Google Play, about 112K on the US App Store).
- **Identity confidence:** Platform-specific. The Android listing (`com.alibaba.aliexpresshd`) and the iOS listing (id436672029) share a name and are a **probable counterpart pair, shown as a verification candidate only**. This run has no official ownership evidence and no user confirmation, so they are not grouped as one product.
- **Coverage:** Found in il, us and gb on both platforms. Each listing came from a separate query.
- **Why spike:** Reviews about slow shipping to Israel, unclear sellers, tracking and returns describe the exact problem Israball would solve. This spike tests the Israel delivery angle most directly.
- **Links:** Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alibaba.aliexpresshd. iOS (verification candidate) https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id436672029

## 3. Wish: Shop and Save (direct)

- **Job/audience overlap:** Same job (cheap gadgets and general goods) and the same budget-driven audience. It works as a general discount marketplace, the model Israball would follow if it goes broad.
- **Discovery evidence:** Store search for "cheap gadgets fast shipping Israel" in il, us and gb. Large ratings base (about 14.4M on Google Play).
- **Identity confidence:** One platform-specific listing (`com.contextlogic.wish`). No iOS counterpart surfaced, and ownership is not verified.
- **Coverage:** Found in il, us and gb, Android only.
- **Why spike:** A cheap-goods marketplace that is not AliExpress or Temu. Its reviews show the trust, quality and delivery-time complaints a curated, fast-delivery Israball would have to answer.
- **Links:** Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.contextlogic.wish. iOS not observed.

## 4. WE BALL (adjacent)

- **Job/audience overlap:** A focused sports/ball store, not a general marketplace. Its audience overlaps if Israball picks the sports niche its name suggests. It is a branded single-store app, which is close to Israball's planned form (branded storefront plus mobile).
- **Discovery evidence:** Store search for "sports gear ball store" in il, us and gb. No ratings count observed.
- **Identity confidence:** One platform-specific listing (`co.tapcart.app.id_tdbfFGZfhc`). The package ID suggests it was built with a hosted store-app builder; that is a hint, not verified ownership. No iOS counterpart surfaced.
- **Coverage:** Found in il, us and gb, Android only.
- **Why spike:** It is the closest example of a niche-branded ball/sports store. Review volume may be very thin, so this spike may mostly confirm that the niche app space is underserved rather than surface rich pain points.
- **Links:** Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.tapcart.app.id_tdbfFGZfhc. iOS not observed.

## 5. Khelmart – Sports Store Online (adjacent)

- **Job/audience overlap:** A multi-brand online sports gear store. It overlaps with a sports-focused Israball but is not a discount/dropship marketplace.
- **Discovery evidence:** Store search for "sports gear ball store" in il, us and gb. No ratings count observed.
- **Identity confidence:** One platform-specific listing (`com.app.khelmarta`). No iOS counterpart surfaced, and ownership is not verified.
- **Coverage:** Found in il, us and gb, Android only.
- **Why spike:** A second sports-niche data point, set against WE BALL. Review evidence is likely thin, the same as for WE BALL.
- **Links:** Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.khelmarta. iOS not observed.

## Considered but not shortlisted

- **eBay** (`com.ebay.mobile`): a broad marketplace. Overlaps on cheap goods, but less on the dropship and fast-to-Israel job. Can replace Wish if a broader cross-border marketplace comparison is wanted.
- **DHgate** (`com.dhgate.buyermob`) and **Alibaba.com** (`com.alibaba.intl.android.apps.poseidon`): mostly wholesale/B2B buyers, so a weaker match for retail shoppers.
- **Trendsi**, **Dropshipping+ Winning Products**, **Shopify**: tools for sellers and store operators, not shopper-facing competitors. They may matter later when building, but not for the competitor spike.

## Gaps and caveats

- No Israeli local shopping app surfaced in the portfolio, even though the "fast delivery to Israel" search covered the il storefront. Local Israeli competitors and website-only players still need checking through their official sites (per the brief's accepted assumptions).
- Shein is named in the brief but did not appear in the prepared portfolio.
- Only the sports niche was searched. The home-gadget and pet-supply niches from brief hypothesis 4 have no candidates yet.
- AliExpress is the only shortlisted product with an iOS listing. Every other entry is Android-only in this run.
- The two sports-niche candidates show no ratings count, so review-based evidence for the sports niche may be sparse.
- Storefront results change over time. This is a bounded sample of three markets, not an exhaustive list.
