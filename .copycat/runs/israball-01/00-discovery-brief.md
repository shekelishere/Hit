# Discovery brief — Israball (approved 2026-11-13)

- **Starting point:** idea; AliExpress is the intended supplier (not a seed app to clone).
- **Decision:** whether Israball is worth building, which niche to focus on, and what makes it meaningfully different from buying directly on AliExpress, Temu, or Shein.
- **Target user:** online shoppers in Israel first, plus English-speaking shoppers.
- **Market scope:** Israel (il; Hebrew, RTL) plus US (us) and UK (gb). Prices in ₪ and $.
- **Niche:** open — research compares candidate niches and recommends one.
- **Product form:** branded website plus mobile version; original name, copy, and design. No reuse of AliExpress branding, listing copy, or photos as Israball's own.
- **Out of scope now:** building, payments, ad spend, supplier or customer contact (each needs separate approval).

## Search hypotheses
1. Category: dropshipping / discount marketplace apps (AliExpress, Temu, Shein and similar).
2. User job: buy cheap gadgets/products online with fast, trackable delivery to Israel (incl. local Israeli shopping apps).
3. Workaround: buying directly from AliExpress and tolerating slow shipping and unclear sellers.
4. Adjacent substitute: niche-focused stores — sports gear (fits the name), home gadgets, pet supplies.

## Accepted assumptions
- Research covers mobile app stores; website-only competitors are checked only through official sites.
- Review evidence is public and sampled, not exhaustive.
